To determine which anime were *actually animated* by Cygames staff (specifically their internal studio, **CygamesPictures**), and not just produced by the parent company, you have to look for the "Animation Production" credit.

While the parent company (Cygames) produces many shows, their subsidiary studio **CygamesPictures** is the one that handles the actual drawing and animation.

Here is the complete breakdown of anime where Cygames' internal studio was directly involved in the animation.

### 1. The *Uma Musume* Franchise (Direct Animation)

You are correct that the main TV series (Seasons 1–3) were outsourced to P.A. Works and Studio KAI. However, CygamesPictures took over for the spin-offs and the movie to ensure higher quality control in-house.

* **Uma Musume: Pretty Derby – Road to the Top** (ONA)
* This 4-episode web series was fully animated by CygamesPictures. It is distinctively higher budget than the TV series.


* **Uma Musume: Pretty Derby – Beginning of a New Era** (Movie)
* The 2024 theatrical film was animated entirely by CygamesPictures.


* **Uma Musume: Cinderella Gray** (TV Anime - Upcoming)
* As you noted, this upcoming adaptation is being animated in-house by CygamesPictures.



### 2. Other Anime Series (Direct Animation)

These are the non-*Uma Musume* shows where CygamesPictures was the primary animation studio.

* **Princess Connect! Re:Dive** (Seasons 1 & 2)
* This is arguably their flagship animation showcase. They handled both seasons in-house to capture the specific look of the game's cutscenes.


* **Mysteria Friends** (Manaria Friends)
* This was the studio's first full TV series production. It is a spin-off of *Rage of Bahamut* (another Cygames IP).


* **The Idolm@ster Cinderella Girls U149**
* CygamesPictures animated this spin-off series focusing on the younger idols (under 149cm tall).


* **Brave Bang Bravern!** (*Yuuki Bakuhatsu Bang Bravern*)
* An original Mecha anime released in 2024. This was a significant project for them as it was an original IP rather than an adaptation of one of their existing gacha games.



### 3. Short Films & Special Projects

* **Blade Runner: Black Out 2022**
* **Note:** This is a major credit. It is a short film prequel to the *Blade Runner 2049* movie. Directed by Shinichirō Watanabe (*Cowboy Bebop*), the animation production was handled by CygamesPictures.



### 4. Upcoming Projects (Announced)

* **Apocalypse Hotel** (Original TV Anime)
* An original series currently in production at CygamesPictures.


* **The Summer Hikaru Died** (*Hikaru ga Shinda Natsu*)
* An adaptation of the popular horror/mystery manga.
